About

I'm a Computer Science & Economics graduate from Colgate University (GPA 3.60, Honors in Computer Science) with a passion for building at the intersection of finance, technology, and product. My background spans product management, financial analysis, software development, and entrepreneurship, allowing me to bridge technical execution with business strategy.

Most recently, I worked at FactSet in New York, where I identified over $500K in potential vendor cost savings, evaluated data coverage across 5,000+ companies, and developed an AI-powered search prototype to improve internal knowledge retrieval and decision-making.

Before FactSet, I founded Mi Pyme Digital, a nonprofit initiative that helped more than 200 small businesses accelerate their digital transformation through websites, automation tools, and growth strategies. Working directly with business owners taught me how to translate technical solutions into measurable outcomes.

I also completed an Honors Thesis on systemic risk in decentralized financial networks, building Monte Carlo simulation models to analyze liquidation risk, oracle mispricing, and contagion effects across DeFi markets.
